### Mosaic Layouts

The mosaic arranges itself from the number of visible tiles: a hero tile plus
supporting tiles that fill the remaining tracks with no empty cells. Anything
past `max` collapses into a `+N` badge on the last tile.

This is a mosaic, not a masonry — every tile is cropped to its cell rather than
sized by its own aspect ratio.
