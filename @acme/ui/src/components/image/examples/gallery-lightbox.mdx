### Gallery Lightbox

`GalleryLightbox` is the full viewer: a looping carousel over mixed
image/video `media`, zoom, rotate, flip, a thumbnail strip, and an optional
`onDownloadAll` action. Drive it from a thumbnail grid — `initialIndex` picks
the slide to land on, and transforms reset every time it opens or the slide
changes.
